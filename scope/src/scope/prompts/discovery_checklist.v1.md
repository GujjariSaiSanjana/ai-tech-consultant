- business_outcome: the measurable result the client wants (revenue, signups, hours saved) and how
  success is judged.
- integrations: every external system (CRM, booking, payments, email, calendars), direction of data,
  who owns the accounts and API access.
- data_migration: what data moves, from where, volume, format, cleanup, what is left behind.
- roles_permissions: who uses the admin, what each role may see and change.
- acceptance_criteria: how each feature will be accepted as done; who tests and signs off.
- non_functional: performance targets, accessibility, privacy/compliance, security, uptime, backups.
- timeline: launch date, fixed deadlines, phases, client-side dependencies.
- budget: budget range or ceiling, payment terms.
- decision_maker: who approves scope and money; who else can veto.
- support: post-launch support, maintenance, training, warranty period.
- out_of_scope: what is explicitly not included this phase.
